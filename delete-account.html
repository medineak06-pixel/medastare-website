What will be deleted

When you delete your MedaStaré account, we will remove:

  • Your profile information (name, email, profile details)
  • Your fashion preferences, style data and personalized recommendations
  • Your saved wardrobe items, looks and collections
  • Your app usage data and account settings

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where required by law, including:

  • Information needed to comply with legal obligations (e.g., fraud prevention, security, regulatory requirements)
  • Aggregated and anonymized data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you
  • Records that must be kept for legitimate business purposes in accordance with applicable laws
