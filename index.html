About MedaStaré Inc.

An independent fashion technology house shaping the future of personal intelligence.

MedaStaré Inc. is a U.S. based fashion technology company creating a connected portfolio of proprietary intelligence products across styling, wardrobe, visual creation, body aware fashion, beauty and digital culture.

Through MedAI™, Closeté™, MedaVision™, EraFusion™, FashionStare™, MedaBody AI™, StyleTrack™, MuseMatch™, MedaStaré Edit™ and MedArena™, the company brings its own technology brands into one refined global ecosystem built around individuality.
