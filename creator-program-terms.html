How the Creator Program works

A consumer purchases a StarGift from MedaStaré through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing flow. The consumer purchase is not a direct cash transfer to the Creator.

An eligible Creator may separately earn a contractual Creator Payout from MedaStaré under these Terms and the Creator Payout Schedule.

The current Schedule uses a 25% Platform & Program Services Fee applied to Net Proceeds. The Platform Fee is MedaStaré's commercial compensation for providing and operating the Creator monetization ecosystem; it is not a deduction from money already owned by the Creator.

1. Acceptance, eligibility and definitions

These Creator Program Terms ("Creator Terms") are an agreement between MedaStaré Inc., a Delaware corporation with a registered address at 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713 ("MedaStaré," "we," "us" or "our"), and the individual or entity enrolling in the StarGift Creator Program ("Creator" or "you").

By enrolling in the Creator Program, accepting these Creator Terms, connecting a supported Stripe Connect payout account, or accepting a Creator Payout, you agree to be bound by these Creator Terms and the Creator Payout Schedule. If you do not agree, do not enroll or accept Creator Payouts.

Eligibility

To enroll and remain eligible, you must:

  • be at least 18 years old, or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction if higher;
  • be located in a country currently supported for Creator Payouts;
  • be able to create and maintain a Stripe Connect account or another payout method MedaStaré expressly supports;
  • successfully complete identity, sanctions and payout verification required by Stripe, MedaStaré or applicable law;
  • provide valid tax documentation when required;
  • maintain a MedaStaré account in good standing; and
  • comply with the Terms of Use, applicable Community Guidelines & Content Policy, these Creator Terms and applicable law.

Defined terms

  • StarGift means the digital virtual item purchased by a consumer inside MedaStaré through the applicable supported app-store billing flow and sent within MedaStaré as an expression of appreciation, recognition or support for an eligible Creator.
  • Store Commission means the actual commission, service fee or equivalent store charge deducted by Apple or Google for the relevant transaction before or as part of settlement to MedaStaré.
  • Net Proceeds means the amount actually credited or remitted to MedaStaré and attributable to the relevant StarGift after Store Commission and any other store-controlled transaction deductions reflected in the applicable settlement.
  • Platform & Program Services Fee or Platform Fee means the percentage of Net Proceeds MedaStaré retains as compensation for providing, operating, maintaining and supporting the StarGift and Creator Program ecosystem, at the rate stated in the Creator Payout Schedule.
  • Creator Payout means the contractual amount MedaStaré calculates as payable to an eligible Creator under Section 3 and the Creator Payout Schedule.
  • Creator Payout Schedule means MedaStaré's then-current published schedule setting the applicable Platform Fee and the timing, reserve, threshold and related payout parameters assigned to that Schedule by these Creator Terms.

2. The StarGift Creator Program

A consumer purchases a StarGift from MedaStaré through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing flow. The applicable store processes the consumer payment according to its own terms and settlement arrangements with MedaStaré.

The StarGift itself is a digital virtual product. It is not cash, electronic money, stored value, a bank deposit, an investment product, a wage, an employment bonus, or a direct peer-to-peer transfer of the consumer's purchase price to you.

The Net Proceeds received or credited to MedaStaré are MedaStaré's revenue. Receiving a StarGift does not give you ownership of the consumer's payment, MedaStaré's store receivable, MedaStaré's revenue, or any specific funds.

Your right, if you satisfy these Creator Terms, is a contractual right to the Creator Payout calculated under these Creator Terms and the Creator Payout Schedule.

The consumer-facing terms applicable to purchasing and sending StarGifts are set out in the StarGift Terms.

3. Creator Payouts

For each eligible StarGift you receive, MedaStaré calculates the Creator Payout using the following formula:

Creator Payout = Net Proceeds × (100% − the Platform Fee then in effect).

The current Platform Fee and worked examples are published in the Creator Payout Schedule. The rate applicable to a StarGift is the rate disclosed and in effect when that StarGift is received, subject to any correction required for fraud, refund, reversal, technical error or applicable law.

The Creator Payout is calculated from Net Proceeds, not from the consumer's gross purchase price. Store Commission rates are determined by the relevant store and may vary by store, program, transaction, territory or other applicable criteria.

If the Store Commission or another store-controlled settlement deduction changes, Net Proceeds and therefore the Creator Payout may change even if the Platform Fee itself does not change.

The Creator Payout is a contractual payment MedaStaré makes from its own revenue for eligible participation in the Creator Program. It is not an assignment of the consumer's payment, not ownership of a pooled fund, not a bank or wallet balance, and not a royalty independent of these Creator Terms.

MedaStaré may offer a different, tiered or promotional Platform Fee to particular Creators, campaigns or markets if the applicable rate is disclosed before it applies to the relevant StarGift.

4. Platform & Program Services Fee

The Platform Fee is MedaStaré's commercial compensation for providing and operating the monetization ecosystem that enables eligible Creators to receive Creator Payouts through StarGift.

Depending on the feature and market, the platform and program services supported by the Platform Fee include:

  • StarGift and Creator monetization infrastructure;
  • platform access, distribution and Creator discovery functionality;
  • content hosting, delivery and related platform operations;
  • moderation and trust-and-safety operations;
  • Creator and customer support;
  • fraud, refund, reversal and chargeback administration;
  • payout operations and Creator-program administration;
  • compliance and risk-management administration; and
  • ongoing development, maintenance and operation of the Creator platform.

The Platform Fee is not a deduction from money already owned by the Creator. The Creator's contractual entitlement is the Creator Payout produced by the disclosed formula after application of the Platform Fee.

The Platform Fee is not represented as reimbursement of MedaStaré's exact costs. MedaStaré does not represent that its costs equal the Platform Fee percentage. The fee is the agreed commercial price for the platform and program services described above.

The Platform Fee is separate from Store Commission, tax withholding, refunds, reversals, chargebacks and other adjustments expressly permitted by these Creator Terms.

5. Pending balances, holds and reserves

A Creator Payout begins as a pending balance and becomes an available balance only after the hold period stated in the Creator Payout Schedule and satisfaction of applicable verification and program requirements.

During the hold period, a Creator Payout may be reduced or removed if the underlying StarGift is refunded, reversed, charged back, determined to be unauthorized, linked to prohibited conduct, or otherwise subject to an adjustment allowed by these Creator Terms.

MedaStaré may apply a reserve in the amount and for the period stated in the Creator Payout Schedule. MedaStaré may apply a longer hold or larger reserve to a specific Creator where reasonably justified by fraud risk, refund or dispute history, verification status or comparable program risk, and will communicate that treatment where legally permitted.

Pending, available and reserved balances are contractual payout calculations. They are not deposits, stored value, trust funds or wallet balances and do not give you a claim to any particular account or funds held by MedaStaré.

6. Payout schedule and minimum threshold

MedaStaré issues eligible available balances according to the payout cadence stated in the Creator Payout Schedule and only after the minimum payout threshold stated there has been met.

If your available balance is below the applicable threshold on a payout date, it rolls forward until the threshold is met, subject to these Creator Terms.

Payouts are issued through the payout destination associated with your supported Stripe Connect account or another method expressly approved by MedaStaré. You are responsible for keeping payout information accurate, current and verified.

MedaStaré is not responsible for delay or misdirection caused by incorrect or incomplete information supplied by you, banking-network delays, Stripe processing, sanctions review, provider outages or other circumstances outside MedaStaré's reasonable control, except to the extent liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Refunds, reversals, chargebacks and offsets

If Apple, Google or another authorized store refunds, reverses or charges back a StarGift transaction, or if the transaction is determined to be unauthorized or fraudulent, the associated Creator Payout may be removed, reduced or reversed to the corresponding extent.

If the affected Creator Payout remains unpaid, MedaStaré may deduct the applicable amount from the pending or available balance. If the Creator Payout has already been paid or the remaining balance is insufficient, MedaStaré may offset the shortfall against future Creator Payouts, net it against other amounts MedaStaré owes you, or recover it through another lawful method.

MedaStaré may also withhold, hold, offset, reverse or recover Creator Payouts reasonably linked to fraud, abuse, self-gifting, related-account manipulation, artificial engagement, violations of these Creator Terms or activity inconsistent with genuine consumer engagement.

Recovery rights under this Section are intended to correct invalid or lost transactions and are not a penalty. MedaStaré will not intentionally recover more than the amount reasonably attributable to the relevant refund, reversal, chargeback, confirmed fraud or other permitted adjustment, except for separately recoverable costs or amounts where expressly permitted by law or agreement.

If MedaStaré determines that particular StarGifts or Creator Payouts resulted from fraud, prohibited self-dealing, manipulation or another violation, MedaStaré may forfeit only the Creator Payouts reasonably connected to that conduct, unless the affected transactions cannot reasonably be separated from a broader fraudulent or violating pattern.

Either party may end Creator Program participation at any time. Termination does not automatically eliminate an otherwise valid Creator Payout already earned and not subject to a permitted hold, offset, reversal or forfeiture.

12. How to dispute a decision about your payouts

If MedaStaré withholds, offsets, reverses or forfeits a Creator Payout, suspends payouts or ends your participation, you may dispute the decision by emailing support@medastare.com within the dispute window stated in the Creator Payout Schedule.

Your dispute should identify the decision, explain why you believe it is incorrect and include relevant non-sensitive supporting information.

MedaStaré will review the dispute and aims to provide a written response within 30 days after receiving the information reasonably necessary to complete the review. An affected balance may remain on hold while the dispute is open.

This internal process does not waive or replace any non-waivable legal right or the dispute provisions in Section 18.

13. Account deletion and unclaimed balances

If you voluntarily delete your MedaStaré account while an eligible Creator balance remains unpaid, the post-deletion verification window and related payout treatment stated in the Creator Payout Schedule apply.

A balance still in an applicable hold period continues through that hold period, subject to refunds, reversals, fraud review, verification and the other rules in these Creator Terms.

If an otherwise valid balance cannot be paid because verification remains incomplete, MedaStaré may continue holding the amount and may ultimately report or remit it as unclaimed property where required by applicable law. MedaStaré does not automatically convert a valid unpaid Creator balance into its own revenue merely because an account was deleted.

Balances lawfully forfeited or reversed under Sections 7 or 11 are governed by those Sections instead.
