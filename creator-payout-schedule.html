How Creator Payouts are calculated

Creator Payout = Net Proceeds × 75%, using the current 25% Platform & Program Services Fee.

Net Proceeds means the amount MedaStaré actually receives for the relevant StarGift after the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play store commission or service fee for that transaction.

The consumer's purchase price is not the Creator Payout base, and the Platform Fee is not a deduction from money already owned by the Creator. Creator rights arise under the Creator Program Terms and this Schedule.

Publication note: the percentage fee is approved at 25%. The timing and threshold fields marked FINAL APPROVAL REQUIRED must be completed before this Schedule is published or incorporated into Creator enrollment.

1. Scope and controlling terms

This Creator Payout Schedule ("Schedule") forms part of the MedaStaré Creator Program Terms and states the current commercial parameters used to calculate and administer Creator Payouts under the StarGift program.

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Creator Program Terms. If this Schedule conflicts with the Creator Program Terms regarding a numerical rate, threshold or timing parameter that the Creator Program Terms expressly assign to this Schedule, this Schedule controls for that parameter.

The version of this Schedule in effect when a StarGift is received determines the Platform Fee and other prospective commercial parameters applicable to that StarGift, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

3. Creator Payout formula

For each eligible StarGift:

  1. the consumer purchases the StarGift through the applicable app-store billing flow;
  2. the applicable store deducts its actual commission, service fee and any amounts it is responsible for deducting or remitting under its rules;
  3. the amount MedaStaré actually receives for the StarGift after the applicable Store Commission becomes the Net Proceeds for purposes of the Creator Program;
  4. MedaStaré retains the 25% Platform & Program Services Fee from Net Proceeds; and
  5. the remaining 75% of Net Proceeds is the Creator Payout before any later adjustment permitted by the Creator Program Terms, including a refund, reversal, chargeback, fraud finding, tax withholding, offset or other expressly authorized adjustment.

The formula is therefore:

Creator Payout = (Consumer Purchase Price − actual applicable Store Commission and store-controlled transaction deductions included in the Net Proceeds calculation) × 75%.

The Creator does not acquire a right to the consumer's gross purchase price, to the Store Commission, or to any specific funds held or received by MedaStaré. The Creator's right is the contractual right to the Creator Payout calculated under the Creator Program Terms and this Schedule.

4. Platform & Program Services Fee

The current Platform & Program Services Fee is 25% of Net Proceeds.

The fee is MedaStaré's commercial compensation for providing, operating, maintaining and supporting the StarGift and Creator monetization ecosystem. The services and infrastructure supported by the fee include, as applicable:

  • StarGift and Creator monetization infrastructure;
  • platform access, distribution and Creator discovery functionality;
  • content hosting, delivery and related platform operations;
  • moderation and trust-and-safety operations;
  • Creator and customer support;
  • fraud, refund, reversal and chargeback administration;
  • Creator payout operations and related program administration;
  • compliance and risk-management administration; and
  • ongoing development, maintenance and operation of the Creator platform.

The Platform Fee is not presented as reimbursement of MedaStaré's exact costs and does not represent that MedaStaré's costs equal 25% of Net Proceeds. It is the agreed commercial price for MedaStaré's platform and program services.

The Platform Fee is separate from any Store Commission charged by Apple or Google and separate from any tax withholding, refund, reversal, chargeback or other adjustment authorized under the Creator Program Terms.

6. Pending balances, holds and reserves

A Creator Payout initially appears as a pending balance and remains subject to the standard hold period stated in Section 2 of this Schedule.

The purpose of a hold is to allow time for transaction validation, store settlement information, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, fraud review, eligibility verification and other program controls permitted by the Creator Program Terms.

If MedaStaré applies a standard reserve, the reserve percentage and additional reserve period will be stated in Section 2 before it is applied as a standard program parameter. A Creator-specific longer hold or larger reserve may be applied only as permitted by the Creator Program Terms, including where justified by fraud risk, dispute history or verification status.

Pending and reserved amounts are contractual payout calculations and are not bank deposits, stored value, trust funds or wallet balances held for the Creator.

7. Payout timing and threshold

MedaStaré issues eligible available balances according to the payout cadence stated in Section 2.

A payout is issued only when the Creator's available balance meets or exceeds the minimum payout threshold stated in Section 2. If the threshold is not met, the available balance rolls forward to a later payout cycle, subject to the Creator Program Terms.

Payouts are made through the Creator's supported Stripe Connect payout destination. The Creator must maintain accurate, complete and verified payout information.

Banking-network timing, Stripe processing, weekends, holidays, sanctions review, verification requirements and other provider or legal processes may affect the date funds actually arrive after MedaStaré initiates a payout.

8. Refunds, reversals and offsets

If an underlying StarGift is refunded, reversed, charged back, determined to be unauthorized or tied to confirmed fraud or prohibited activity, the associated Creator Payout may be reduced, removed, reversed, withheld, offset or recovered as provided in the Creator Program Terms.

A reversal is not treated as an additional Platform Fee or penalty. It reflects the loss or invalidation of the underlying transaction or another adjustment expressly permitted by the Creator Program Terms.

If a paid-out amount becomes recoverable, MedaStaré may offset that amount against future Creator Payouts or use another recovery method permitted by the Creator Program Terms and applicable law. The de minimis recovery threshold stated in Section 2 applies where the Creator Program Terms provide for it.

9. Payout disputes

A Creator who disputes a withholding, offset, reversal, forfeiture, payout suspension or other covered payout decision must submit the dispute within the dispute window stated in Section 2, measured from the date MedaStaré notifies the Creator of the decision.

The dispute process, review standard and response timing are governed by the Creator Program Terms. The Schedule sets only the applicable submission window.

10. Account deletion and verification

If a Creator voluntarily deletes a MedaStaré account while an eligible balance remains unpaid, the post-deletion verification window stated in Section 2 applies for completing any outstanding Stripe verification and receiving an eligible final payout, subject to the Creator Program Terms.

Amounts that cannot lawfully be paid or remain unclaimed after the applicable period are handled under the Creator Program Terms and applicable unclaimed-property law. MedaStaré does not automatically convert an otherwise valid unpaid Creator balance into its own revenue merely because the Creator account was deleted.

11. Changes to this Schedule

MedaStaré may change this Schedule prospectively as permitted by the Creator Program Terms. A material change to the Platform Fee, hold period, reserve, payout cadence, minimum threshold or another material commercial parameter will be communicated in accordance with the notice requirements in the Creator Program Terms.

A prospective change does not retroactively reduce a Creator Payout already calculated for a StarGift received before the effective date of that change, except where an adjustment is separately permitted for refund, reversal, fraud, legal compliance or another reason under the Creator Program Terms.
