Community Guidelines

Rules for MedaStaré social, challenge and user generated content features when those features are enabled.

Effective August 5, 2026Version 1.0MedaStaré Inc.
On this page1. Respect and safety2. Authenticity3. Rights and content4. Enforcement5. User controls6. Appeals

Activation status

These Guidelines apply when MedaStaré enables public profiles, posts, direct messaging, MedArena sharing, StyleMatch social functions or another user content community feature. Unfinished social features must remain disabled in the production application.

1. Respect and safety

  • Do not threaten, harass, stalk, shame, bully or target another person.
  • Do not promote hatred or discrimination based on protected characteristics.
  • Do not sexualize or exploit minors.
  • Do not share nonconsensual intimate material, private information or another person location.
  • Do not encourage self harm, eating disorder behavior, dangerous challenges or violence.

2. Authenticity

  • Do not impersonate a person, celebrity, brand, agency or MedaStaré representative.
  • Do not use generated media to deceive people about a real event or endorsement.
  • Do not manipulate challenges, rankings, rewards or engagement through fake accounts or automation.
  • Disclose paid or gifted commercial content where law or platform rules require it.

3. Rights and content

  • Post only content you have the right to share.
  • Respect copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity and image rights.
  • Do not upload a person photo for public use without the required permission.
  • Do not present a generated style output as an authorized celebrity or brand collaboration.

4. Enforcement

MedaStaré may warn, restrict, remove content, limit visibility, suspend or terminate accounts based on severity, history, risk and law. Content may be preserved when required for safety, appeals, legal requests or evidence.

5. User controls

Enabled community features must provide content reporting, user reporting, blocking and an appropriate moderation process. Users should use in app controls for immediate protection and contact support for unresolved safety issues.

6. Appeals

Where an appeal is available, send the relevant reference to support@medastare.com. An appeal does not guarantee restoration.
