3. Eligibility, participant cap and submission requirements

Eligible entrants must be at least eighteen (18) years old, hold an eligible active MedaStaré membership (Star, Star VIP or Crown, as applicable), reside in a jurisdiction activated for this Cycle, accept the governing competition terms and meet all other requirements of the Master Rules. App availability or membership alone does not confer competition or cash-prize eligibility.

Overall participant cap: No fixed numerical cap is proposed. Submission limit: One eligible entry per person/account for this Edition, subject to the application's applicable rules.

Submissions must be original photos to which the entrant has the necessary rights, showing an identifiable outfit or look. Files must meet the entry interface's technical rules, including supported JPEG, PNG or WebP formats and applicable file-size requirements. Prohibited manipulation, impersonation, duplicate entries, or unauthorized third-party content may result in disqualification.

6. Assessment, shortlist and final judging

Preliminary qualifying standard: At least 80/100 on the MedaVision automated skill-and-merit assessment. Finalist shortlist: Up to twenty (20) highest-ranked eligible entries meeting the qualifying threshold. Lower-scoring entries will not be added to fill empty shortlist spots.

Ranked winners: Five (5), subject to sufficient eligible and qualified finalist entries and applicable published insufficiency rules. The named human Judging Panel reviews shortlisted entries and applies disclosed criteria. The currently implemented ranking formula uses a weighted combination of 70% AI score and 30% human jury score; its legal and editorial consistency with the Master Rules requires approval before this Annex is effective.

Named Judging Panel: [TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE PUBLICATION]. Manual scoring criteria, relative weights, merit-based tie-break hierarchy, and insufficient-qualifier handling: [TO BE CONFIRMED AND PUBLISHED BEFORE THIS ANNEX BECOMES EFFECTIVE]. There shall be no random tie-break or randomly selected winner.

7. Winner notification, verification and prize fulfillment

Potential winners may be notified in-app and/or at their verified account email. Under the Master Rules, a potential winner ordinarily has ten (10) business days to respond, with a possible five (5) additional business-day cure period when permitted.

Prize fulfillment requires successful identity, age, residence and eligibility verification; receipt of necessary documentation; and completion of applicable MedaStaré and jurisdiction-specific legal, regulatory, tax, banking and payment reviews. A proposed or final competition ranking is not a substitute for lawful prize clearance.

Following completion of all required verification and clearance prerequisites, MedaStaré will use commercially reasonable efforts to initiate or complete lawful prize fulfillment within thirty (30) calendar days, subject to the Master Rules and mandatory local law. Necessary regulatory authorizations, local counsel review, tax processing or banking compliance may reasonably extend that period. MedaStaré should provide status information to affected winners when a material delay occurs.

A prize that cannot legally be transferred must be handled under the Master Rules, including permitted alternative fulfillment or substitution, and not as an arbitrary refusal to honor a valid prize.

8. Scoring, judging, tie-breaks and insufficient qualifiers

Automated qualification: Only eligible entries assessed by MedaVision at 80/100 or higher can enter the highest-scoring shortlist of up to 20. Fewer than 20 qualifying entries means a shorter shortlist; the threshold must not be lowered to fill places. Material criteria, methodology, limitations and permitted human error correction must be stated before the effective rules are accepted.

Human judging: Each shortlisted eligible entry must receive a documented assessment from unconflicted named judges using public objective, skill-based criteria. The currently implemented ranking formula is 70% preliminary AI score plus 30% mean human jury score, rather than a wholly discretionary human award. Individual juror scores, aggregate scores, score weights, conflicts and ranking approvals must be recorded. The names of the judges and actual jury scoring rubric remain [TO BE VERIFIED AND COMPLETED BY COUNSEL AND PRODUCT TEAM].

Material tie-break defect: The application currently resolves otherwise equal scores using a submission creation timestamp after AI-score comparison. The Master Rules forbid non-merit-based final tie-breaks; earlier submission is not in itself fashion skill. Before effective publication, the ranking code and written terms must use a predetermined merit-only hierarchy, for example: (1) greater jury-assessed originality and creativity; (2) greater jury-assessed styling execution; (3) greater jury-assessed theme interpretation; and (4) additional documented scoring by an independent unconflicted judge under the same rubric. These criteria are proposed pending implementation, not a representation of current production behavior.

Fewer than five qualified finalists: The current results software requires five scored placements. It needs an independently approved, legally disclosed, implemented insufficient-qualifier procedure before cash prizes can be activated. The sponsor shall not invent winning entries, select randomly, lower thresholds post-entry or misrepresent the number of awards actually available. Any unawarded prize disposition and legally required entrant remedies must be fixed and communicated before the final version of this Annex is effective.

Second approval: Final results should be independently confirmed by a second authorized administrator against a tamper-evident scoring, ranking and conflict-resolution record. A ranking shown before final confirmation and verification is provisional. Human review is available for demonstrable technical or integrity errors; subjective dissatisfaction alone does not create an automatic entitlement to rescore.

9. Original photography, fair judging and anti-fraud

Each participant may submit one authorized original fashion look per edition. Uploading content without rights, identifying other persons without required consent, materially manipulating an outfit's appearance, prohibited synthetic or AI-generated contestant photos, fake identities, bots, multiple-account entries, purchased judging favors, undisclosed judge conflicts or attempted interference is prohibited where this restriction was adequately disclosed to entrants. Ordinary lighting, crop and color correction may be permitted under the in-app published photo rules provided they do not materially misrepresent the real outfit.

Employees, administrators, judges, officers and directors of MedaStaré; their immediate families and households; and compensated promoters of this specific Cycle must be assessed and excluded where required under the effective eligibility rules. Conflicted judges must recuse themselves or undergo a documented independent review protocol. Follower numbers, public votes, StarGifts, purchases, advertiser payments and media reach must have no bearing on qualifying or winning scores.

An algorithmic risk flag alone does not establish fraud. Any material adverse action should use proportionate human investigation and a written, consistent decision record, subject to applicable law. An entry photograph submitted for the contest is distinct from the Sponsor's AI-generated promotional artwork.

13. Delays, cancellation, rules changes and non-waivable rights

After identity and jurisdiction-specific clearance are completed, the Master Rules specify commercially reasonable efforts to initiate or complete prize fulfillment within thirty calendar days. Compelled regulatory filing, local-counsel review, tax processing, banking, sanctions screening or other documented circumstances outside reasonable sponsor control may reasonably delay fulfillment; affected winners should be updated on material delays when lawful and practicable. This is not an unlimited right to defer an otherwise valid payment and does not displace any mandatory deadline.

A lawful documented technical incident, security compromise, conflict of interest, materially mistaken scoring or regulatory intervention may justify fair, proportionate remedial actions under the Master Rules. No entry period, stated award amount, eligibility requirement or selection rule may be materially changed retrospectively for convenience or to avoid prize costs. Required material amendments must be versioned, notified appropriately, supported by applicable law and accompanied by any mandated refunds, withdrawal opportunities or other remedies. A cancellation or disqualification does not extinguish rights that cannot legally be waived.

MedaStaré may not arbitrarily reduce a stated prize, deny a verified lawful winner without an objective basis, choose an alternative winner randomly or hold payment indefinitely. Where lawful fulfillment of a specific prize is impossible, the documented substitute or next-ranked eligible recipient rules in the Master Rules apply, subject to fair dealing and mandatory consumer rights. Competitors may retain their historical ranking as required or permitted even when lawful prize fulfillment differs.

To the fullest extent allowed by law, the sponsor is not responsible for certain third-party technical disruptions beyond its reasonable control, but nothing in this Annex excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, excuses the sponsor's own unlawful practices, or prevents complaints to a regulator or a court. Unenforceable provisions must be limited only to the degree allowed by mandatory local law.
