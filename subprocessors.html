Feature dependent providers

A provider is used only when the relevant feature and production configuration are active. The app identifies the actual AI provider before a qualifying transfer. This list must be reviewed whenever a provider or contract changes.

Named providers and provider categories are maintained according to the active production configuration. Infrastructure providers disclosed by category will be replaced or supplemented with their legal names before their processing becomes material to a user disclosure or where applicable law requires named disclosure. The app identifies the actual external AI provider before a qualifying transfer.

2. Provider roles

A provider may act as a processor or service provider for MedaStaré, or as an independent controller for its own platform obligations. Apple may act independently for App Store billing and account services. The applicable provider terms determine the exact role.

3. Safeguards

  • Purpose limitation and confidentiality terms where available.
  • Access restrictions and security review proportionate to the service.
  • International transfer measures where applicable.
  • Deletion or return obligations at the end of service.
  • Incident notification and cooperation obligations where appropriate.
  • No authorization to use private app content for unrelated advertising.

4. Changes to providers

MedaStaré may replace a provider for security, performance, availability, legal or commercial reasons. A material AI change that introduces a new recipient, data category or purpose is disclosed before the next qualifying transfer and renewed consent is obtained where required.

5. Questions

Questions about providers may be sent to support@medastare.com.
