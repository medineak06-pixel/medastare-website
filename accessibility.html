Accessibility

MedaStaré accessibility commitments, current approach and feedback channel.

Commitment

MedaStaré aims to make its public information and application usable with assistive technologies and accessible interaction patterns. Accessibility is an ongoing product requirement.

1. Accessibility approach

  • Support Dynamic Type and readable text scaling in the application where technically practical.
  • Provide meaningful accessibility labels for controls and images that convey information.
  • Maintain keyboard, VoiceOver and focus order support for key flows.
  • Avoid relying only on color to communicate status.
  • Respect reduced motion preferences where possible.
  • Provide clear errors, confirmations and alternatives for denied permissions.
  • Keep legal pages structured with headings, lists, tables and visible focus states.

2. Known limitations

Some advanced visual generation, image analysis, three dimensional previews or cinematic effects may have accessibility limitations. MedaStaré seeks to provide text descriptions, alternative controls or a simpler flow where practical.

3. Accessibility feedback

Accessibility feedback may be sent to support@medastare.com. Include the page or feature, assistive technology, device and the barrier encountered. Do not include private account content unless necessary.
