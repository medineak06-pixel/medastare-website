4. Age, residence and account eligibility

Applicants must (a) be natural persons aged at least 18 years; (b) be lawful residents of a specifically activated jurisdiction when entering and when verification is required; (c) meet all properly disclosed membership conditions that have been legally approved in that jurisdiction; (d) use an account in good standing and comply with the Master Rules; (e) use genuine information and complete the identity and eligibility checks reasonably required; and (f) be the lawful creator/rightsholder of their submission or have all required rights and consents.

Excluded persons: employees, officers, directors, the named judges, anyone with direct influence over StarDrop administration or scoring, and their immediate family or household members; additionally, paid creators and agencies engaged to promote this Cycle are excluded where their involvement creates an actual or perceived material conflict. Material relationships with a judge, sponsor, promoter, contractor or entry must be disclosed and assessed. A connected user is not automatically entitled to a prize.

Use of false residence details, VPNs to conceal location, duplicate accounts or identity substitution does not create eligibility. Reasonable good-faith corrections to account data and automated age/profile errors must be evaluated rather than automatically treated as fraud.

6. United States: state-specific conditions and non-automatic clearance

Possible U.S. participation and cash-prize payment are subject to each applicable state’s laws and any applicable federal rules. Sponsor must obtain independent counsel advice on skill-contest classification and whether the paid-membership access model constitutes prohibited consideration, wagering, an unlawful gambling activity or an unfair promotion. Requirements may include exclusions, alternate free entry methods where legally suitable and actually implemented, advance notices, rules filings, prize security/bonds, financial reporting, tax forms, identity verification and payout restrictions.

Florida, New York, Rhode Island and other potentially relevant states require specific scrutiny. Threshold-based game-promotion filing rules must not be assumed to apply to a purely skill-based contest in every instance, nor may the absence of chance be treated as an automatic exemption from all local rules. No state is asserted to be cleared solely by this statement. Never claim a free entry route, state permit, licence or registration unless it has in fact been put in place.

7. Singapore: conditional lawful activation only

Singapore activation is contingent on a written, jurisdiction-specific review of the Gambling Control Act 2022 and subordinate rules, the distinction between genuine skill competitions and chance-based promotions, any paid-membership or participation consideration, applicable authorizations/class-licence eligibility, consumer notices, tax and PDPA obligations. The existence of a class-licence regime does not establish that StarDrop qualifies for it; no Singapore licence or permission is claimed here.

Where local regulation or payment-provider checks require additional time, affected winners will be told what information is needed and, when reasonably possible, the expected next review date. A regulatory hold is not an arbitrary cancellation of a valid competition ranking. Sponsor will not remit funds contrary to mandatory local law.

8. Membership restrictions, pricing and consumer rights

Proposed eligible membership categories: Star, Star VIP and Crown, only to the extent lawfully approved and actually enabled for this Cycle. No tier improves AI scoring, jury review, odds, submission rank or access to extra entries for this Cycle. StellarOrb, StarGift, donations, influencer spend, in-app purchases and product purchases must not influence winner determination.

Any change in subscription access, refunds, cancellation and renewal remains governed by the applicable subscription agreement and mandatory law. No wording here waives refunds, chargeback rights or other mandatory consumer protections. If counsel concludes the current paid-subscription gate cannot lawfully support this competition, the Sponsor must change the actual product flow prospectively or keep that jurisdiction inactive; a disclaimer cannot fix the software’s conduct.

9. Submission mechanics and participant limits

Entry cap: no predetermined total-participant cap is proposed. Per-person limit: one valid entry for this Edition. Only a successfully acknowledged official submission through the designated StarDrop entry form is an Entry. Uploading an image to Arena Lab or receiving an AI pre-check does not enter the competition. Entry timestamps, accepted version of the rules, registered account, receipt and relevant audit records should be retained as prescribed by the Master Rules and privacy law.

Subject to the actual app upload limits, entrants should submit a clear outfit photograph (full body or from the waist up) to which they own all rights. Supported content types are JPEG, PNG and WebP. Photos may be rejected for unlawful content, unauthorized likenesses, misleading AI fabrication, materially deceptive alteration, fraud, prohibited unsafe material, infringement or an inability to review the style fairly. Any technical size and resolution requirements must match the live entry interface; if they differ, the operator must correct the disclosure before accepting submissions.

10. Content ownership and permissions

Entrants retain ownership of their original work. To administer, securely store, moderate, evaluate, audit and lawfully announce outcomes, entrants grant Sponsor the limited, non-exclusive permissions described in the Master Rules and relevant privacy notice. Promotional reuse, winner publicity, third-party image rights and any wider commercial licence remain subject to valid disclosures, any necessary additional consent and mandatory local law. Nothing transfers the creator’s copyright merely by entering. Sponsor may request evidence of ownership and consent and act on justified takedown complaints.

11. AI qualification, human jury and ranking weights

StarDrop is represented as a skill- and merit-based competition without a random drawing. The proposal reflected in current application source is an AI-based qualification score of 80/100, followed by up to 20 eligible high-scoring finalists. No entry scoring below 80 is added solely to fill vacant finalist places.

Proposed preliminary scoring dimensions: Creativity 25%; Theme Match 25%; Color Harmony 25%; Styling Intelligence 25%. No popular vote, follower count, StarGift expenditure or subscription spending contributes to these scores. Qualifying scores are a technical preliminary assessment, not a guarantee of a prize.

Final ranking: Current code combines a 70% AI score with 30% average human jury score for finalists reviewed by the named panel. Each final ranked entrant must receive a genuine human jury assessment; no purely unreviewed AI submission can receive a ranked prize. Before any effective launch, counsel and engineering must confirm that this formula and the human panel's role match the Master Rules' promise of human-determined final winners. The criteria must not be changed selectively for entrants after the deadline.

12. Merit-only tie-breaking and adjudication

The final effective rules must publish and technically implement a predetermined, exclusively merit-based tie-break hierarchy before entries are accepted. Proposed hierarchy for counsel/engineering review: (1) higher final weighted score; (2) higher independently scored Creativity score; (3) higher Theme Match; (4) higher Styling Intelligence; (5) higher Color Harmony; (6) documented independent human re-evaluation of genuinely tied entries using the same rubric and recorded rationale.

Critical implementation discrepancy: Current repository code uses earlier submission timestamp after tied weighted and AI totals. Because the Master Rules require a merit-based tie-break and prohibit random selection, this draft is not effective until the code and published rubric are reconciled. The entrant's wealth, paid tier, upload order, demographic traits, friendship, popularity and chance must never be employed as concealed ranking criteria.

13. Insufficient qualifying entries; vacancies; no sham winners

If fewer than twenty entries meet the qualifying score and eligibility requirements, the actual qualifying subset forms the finalist shortlist. If fewer than five eligible entries remain after legitimate eligibility, fraud and jury review, Sponsor must not invent, randomly choose, buy or substitute unqualified winners simply to complete the five slots. Prizes will be assigned only to genuinely qualified published ranked places, subject to the final disclosed insufficient-qualifier terms and any overriding legal obligation.

Final effective disclosure still required: counsel must approve whether and how any unawarded rank is treated and whether consumer advertising requires further remedy. The advertised USD 18,500 describes the proposed combined value of the five ranked prizes, not a promise that an unqualified or prohibited entry will receive cash. Sponsor must not describe all five awards as “guaranteed” if the applicable rules condition them on verified qualifying entries.

15. Judicial integrity, recusal and anti-manipulation

Judges must be identified in the final Annex, apply published criteria uniformly and disclose material personal, professional and financial relationships with entrants or campaign promoters. A conflicted judge must recuse themself, with recorded replacement or independent review. No entrant, employee, promoter or agency can purchase votes, influence scores, coordinate sham accounts, stage false engagement or make undisclosed arrangements to alter selection. Scores, anomalous entries, recusals and outcome approvals should be logged for independent audit.

16. Verification, identity, sanctions and secure documentation

Potential winners receive an in-app and/or verified-email notification after ranking, followed by proportionate and secure identity, age, residence, tax, ownership, source-of-funds where legally relevant, anti-money-laundering, sanctions, payout and legal-clearance checks. Sponsor may request government identity proof, local tax documents, a limited eligibility affidavit and documents genuinely needed for lawful transfer. Sponsor must not request passwords, account recovery codes or CVC numbers as prize proof.

Names and documents should be shared through approved private channels, kept no longer than lawfully required and not publicly disclosed except where permitted. False eligibility statements, unlawful sanctions status, identity mismatch, established cheating or unjustified failure to cooperate after fair notice may result in disqualification, subject to documented review and mandatory rights.

17. Notification response, cure period and fair review

Initial response window: ten (10) business days after valid delivery of the potential-winner notice, subject to mandatory law. Sponsor may allow a further five (5) business days to correct a remediable verification deficiency when practicable and permitted. A material service outage or Sponsor-caused notification failure should be reviewed fairly before deeming the person nonresponsive.

Affected entrants may request a manual review of a demonstrable age, eligibility, technical, scoring or conflict-of-interest error by contacting support@medastare.com, identifying the competition edition and the specific issue. The Sponsor will document the request and respond in a reasonable period consistent with applicable law. Appeals do not create an automatic entitlement to bypass qualification or disclose other entrants’ confidential information.

18. Cash payments and documented reasonable delays

Once a winner is verified and all legitimately applicable jurisdictional and payment prerequisites have been completed, Sponsor will use commercially reasonable efforts to initiate or complete lawful fulfillment within 30 calendar days, subject to any shorter mandatory legal deadlines or specific published terms. A licensed bank or payment provider may be used. Sponsor may make required tax withholding and issue legally required reports. Lawful bank fees, foreign-exchange treatment and whether the winner receives a USD or local-currency settlement should be disclosed before transfer as required by law.

United States: federal/state tax forms, state eligibility, payment-rail screening and any required registrations or bonds may affect timing. Singapore: counsel review, regulatory categorization, PDPA and tax requirements, and lawful bank transfer restrictions may affect timing. In all cases, a delay must be tied to a genuine outstanding compliance requirement, not used to avoid a lawful prize obligation. Where practicable, Sponsor should provide a material-delay notice and subsequent review update. This provision does not authorize infinite delays or override an enforceable payment deadline.

19. Unlawful fulfillment, preserved ranking and lawful alternatives

Competition results and entitlement to a particular payment are separate only to the extent permitted by law. When a ranked entrant has a genuine legal restriction against receipt, Sponsor will document the reason, assess whether a lawful equal-or-higher-value substitute or alternative method is available and preserve the honest historical result where legally permissible. If rules and applicable law permit, a prize may instead be offered to the next-highest-ranked verified eligible entrant, never to a random nominee. No lower-value unilateral substitution is allowed where prohibited. Sponsor must clearly distinguish ranked winner, verified winner and actual prize recipient in communications or public lists.

Jurisdictional review should occur before activation so this clause does not become a pretext to promote unavailable cash prizes. Sponsor retains all obligations that are mandatory under advertising, gaming/contest, tax and consumer-protection law.

20. Technical failures, fraud, corrections and records

Entries that never reach the official submission endpoint may be ineligible, but proven Sponsor-controlled outages, missing or corrupted audit records and systemic scoring failures require a documented fairness review and any appropriate lawful remedy. Sponsor may suspend affected functionality, investigate and preserve evidence, but must not use a broad limitation of liability to excuse fraudulent, intentional or legally nonwaivable conduct.

Duplicate photos, manipulated images, bot-generated accounts, fabricated identities, paid scoring influence and intellectual-property infringement may be investigated on objective evidence. Proportionate decisions should be logged and affected persons afforded the review required by law. Material competition-record categories should include timestamped rule snapshots, consents, submissions, age/country checks, AI versions and scores, jury evaluations, conflict logs, final rankings, notifications, payouts and incident corrections.

21. Regulatory intervention, suspension, postponement or cancellation

Where an actual security incident, force majeure event, regulatory order, material fraud or demonstrable integrity failure makes further lawful conduct impossible or materially unfair, Sponsor may take only proportionate, legally permissible corrective measures and must preserve applicable consumer rights. Any cancellation, suspension, date change, revised scoring or prize substitution that materially affects existing entrants must be vetted by counsel, recorded with reasons and communicated as required by law. Sponsor must not retrospectively reduce the published cash pool, change qualifications arbitrarily or turn a skill competition into a random draw.

22. Privacy, evidence retention and public winner disclosures

Personal information will be processed for operating the competition, anti-fraud screening, legal and tax compliance, dispute handling and fulfillment as disclosed in the Privacy Policy and applicable local notices. Identity records and payment instructions must not be displayed publicly. Winner username, submitted look, lawful public recognition and any official winners list remain subject to proper notice, applicable privacy rights, third-party rights and required consent. Cross-border data transfers and Singapore PDPA obligations require actual operational compliance, not just notice language.

Official rules versions must remain retrievable for dispute resolution and legally mandated retention, subject to reasonable privacy safeguards, deletion policies and lawful access controls.

23. Final results, disqualification and correction standards

A provisional ranking is not a completed award. Final published outcomes should be confirmed through documented human evaluation and the system's configured dual-approval or other announced governance controls. Any post-publication correction should be limited to demonstrable errors, disqualifying fraud, legal obligations or other grounds genuinely authorized by effective rules. Sponsor should publish a corrected result record with a revision timestamp, without quietly overwriting the original. No entrant can require a judge's private deliberations or other contestants' personal data except as mandated by law.

StarDrop is sponsored and administered solely by MedaStaré Inc., not by Apple Inc., Google LLC, the App Store, Google Play, an AI vendor, payment service provider, bank or promotional creator. Apple has not offered, reviewed or approved these prize terms by virtue of App Store distribution. Required official rules must be visible in the application. The Sponsor must check applicable platform contest, gambling, payments, app-review and in-app purchase rules before making the functionality available.

25. Mandatory claim accuracy and creator advertising notices

Promotional copy must not imply a prize is guaranteed or payable in a country, U.S. state or territory that is not activated. When referring to the proposed Grand Prize, promotions must prominently state that cash-prize eligibility varies by jurisdiction and point to the official current rules and applicable jurisdiction matrix. The Sponsor and contracted creators must not promise fixed odds, unconditional payouts, guaranteed winner counts despite insufficient qualified entries, or approvals that have not been obtained. Local influencer advertising and sponsorship disclosure rules also apply.

Recommended short notice: “USD $10,000 Grand Prize. Cash-prize eligibility varies by country, region and U.S. state. Entry and payout are subject to the current StarDrop Official Rules, effective Cycle Annex, and applicable legal clearance. Void where prohibited.” This draft wording must be checked against local law and the real user flow before public distribution.

26. Disputes, law, severability and enforceable limitations

Subject to mandatory local law, affected persons may send an identified dispute or complaint to support@medastare.com for a documented review. Applicable governing law, mandatory forum and consumer remedies are determined by effective jurisdiction-specific terms and nonwaivable laws; this draft does not impose an unreviewed class-action waiver, arbitration clause, non-disparagement requirement or impossible foreign forum.

Any legal limitation, exclusion, indemnity or remedy restriction applies only to the maximum enforceable extent and cannot eliminate fraud liability, nonwaivable consumer guarantees, earned statutory rights or remedies arising from Sponsor’s own unlawful actions. If a provision is invalid, the remainder continues only insofar as consistent with the competition's lawful purpose and participant fairness. A valid jurisdiction-specific addendum and mandatory law prevail over this Annex.
