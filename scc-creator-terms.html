Important: proposed reward schedule

The illustrative reward amounts below are not an active public offer unless a dated SCC campaign or creator dashboard expressly activates them. Enrollment, approval, attribution, payout and country eligibility conditions apply. No guaranteed income.

1. Program and scope

StarDay Creator Circle (SCC) is a discretionary, application-based creator referral and content reward program administered by MedaStaré Inc. Participation is separate from ordinary MedaStaré membership and does not establish employment, agency, partnership or authority to bind MedaStaré. Only approved creators with an activated account and program code may participate.

2. Eligibility and geographic availability

Applicants must be legally eligible to contract and receive compensation in their place of residence, ordinarily at least 18 years old, and must provide accurate identity, country and tax information. Applications and payouts may be unavailable or restricted in certain jurisdictions. Worldwide app availability does not guarantee SCC eligibility. MedaStaré may require identity, sanctions and payment compliance checks.

3. Application, code and approval

Creators apply through an authorized enrollment flow. Issuing an application or activation code does not guarantee admission or payment. Activation requires account verification and explicit approval. One person may not operate duplicate accounts or self-refer to obtain rewards. Codes must not be sold or transferred.

4. Reward schedule — proposed and subject to activation

The following figures reflect the planned SCC incentive schedule and are NOT an unconditional public offer until the SCC dashboard or a dated, accepted campaign schedule explicitly activates them: verified new user referral USD 0.25; approved original StarDay video USD 1.00; qualifying creator invitation USD 0.20; referred creator approved and activated USD 6.00; first eligible paid Star subscription USD 3.99; VIP USD 5.99; Crown USD 9.99. Rates, qualifying events, caps and currencies must be displayed before the applicable activity. No retroactive rate reduction for already verified eligible activity except corrections for fraud, error or invalid transactions.

5. Qualification, attribution and reversal

Rewards require authentic, attributable actions and internal verification. Downloads, views, clicks, trial starts, duplicate registrations, refunded purchases, chargebacks, fraudulent activity and self-referrals do not automatically qualify. Subscription rewards apply to the first eligible paid transaction only; renewals and upgrades do not create additional commission unless a separate published campaign expressly says so. A reasonable verification and dispute process applies. Do not promise a payment until it is approved and payable.

6. Creator tiers and StarGift

Planned tiers are Starter, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Founder Circle. Tier points and benefits are only effective when published in the authenticated SCC dashboard or accepted campaign terms; examples previously contemplated include one point for an approved referred user and five for an approved creator. StarGift-related platform fees or creator revenue shares are separate from SCC referral rewards. Any applicable 5% fee or other rate must be expressly shown in the relevant transaction terms; it is not guaranteed by this document.

7. Payouts, minimums and tax

Approved earnings are payable only after applicable verification, identity, tax and payout onboarding, and the published settlement period. Payment methods, minimum payout thresholds, currency conversion, fees, withholding and country restrictions will be shown in the SCC dashboard or campaign schedule before earning activity. MedaStaré may delay disputed or legally restricted payouts with notice where lawful. Creators are responsible for their own tax obligations except withholding legally required of MedaStaré.

8. Content rights and creator responsibilities

Creators retain ownership of their original content. By submitting approved program content, they grant MedaStaré a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, display, reproduce and distribute that content within the platform and for program promotion for the period and scope specified in the applicable upload terms. Any paid-ad usage, exclusivity, perpetual license or third-party sublicensing requires separate express permission. Creators must have necessary music, image, likeness and brand permissions and follow platform moderation and IP policies.

9. Advertising disclosures and prohibited practices

Creators must clearly disclose material connections and compensation in each endorsement where required by local advertising law and platform policies (for example #ad or equivalent clear local-language notice). No fake followers, purchased engagement, misleading product claims, impersonation, spam, bot activity, manipulated attribution, undisclosed AI-generated endorsements or illegal content. Creators may not imply that MedaStaré guarantees product safety, authenticity, medical results or income.

10. Privacy, AI and cross-border processing

SCC may process account identifiers, referral attribution, content metrics, payout and tax information, fraud signals and support communications. The Privacy Policy and AI Data Processing notice apply as relevant. Do not upload third-party personal data without appropriate rights. Cross-border data transfers and retention must follow applicable local laws and disclosed safeguards.

11. Suspension, termination and appeals

MedaStaré may suspend or terminate SCC access for documented fraud, material breach, legal restrictions or program discontinuation, with notice and review where required by law. Legitimately earned, verified, payable balances are not automatically forfeited merely because the program closes. Creators may contest a rejected reward through the official support channel and request a human review; evidence and response deadlines should be published operationally.

12. Changes, precedence and effective date

Version 1.0, prepared October 28, 2026. This document is a proposed public SCC framework and must not be treated as activated reward rates or an executed agreement until published and accepted in the SCC enrollment flow. Mandatory local law prevails. Any dated campaign schedule and the creator's accepted SCC agreement govern specific rates and activities. Material changes apply prospectively with notice where required. Prior versions and accepted rates should be archived.
