Cookie Policy

How the MedaStaré website uses necessary delivery technology and how future analytics or marketing technologies will be controlled.

Effective August 5, 2026Version 1.0MedaStaré Inc.

Current website position

This version of the public website does not include advertising cookies in its source code. Necessary delivery logs and limited technical storage may still be used. Nonessential analytics will require an appropriate choice where law requires consent.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies, local storage, pixels, SDK identifiers and similar technologies can store or read information on a browser or device. The mobile application uses device and SDK mechanisms rather than browser cookies for many functions.

2. Categories

CategoryPurposeChoice
Strictly necessarySecurity, network delivery, fraud prevention, consent storage and essential page functions.Used where necessary to provide the site.
PreferencesRemember language or a user selected display preference.Used only when the preference is requested or consent is required.
AnalyticsMeasure visits, performance and product interaction.Not activated in consent jurisdictions until the required choice is available.
MarketingAdvertising measurement or targeted advertising.Not used in the current public build.

3. Server logs

Hosting and security providers may receive IP address, browser, timestamp, request path and technical information to deliver and protect the website. These records are not advertising cookies and are retained according to security and operational needs.

4. Controls

Where a consent manager is introduced, Accept and Reject choices will be equally accessible and users will be able to change preferences later. Browser settings may also block or delete cookies, although essential functions may be affected.

5. Browser privacy signals

MedaStaré will evaluate legally recognized opt out preference signals when a processing activity covered by such a signal is introduced. The current website does not sell personal data or use private app content for cross context behavioral advertising.

6. Changes

This page will be updated before nonessential analytics, advertising pixels or materially different storage technologies are enabled.

7. Contact

Cookie questions may be sent to support@medastare.com.
