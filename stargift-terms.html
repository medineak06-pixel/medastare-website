A StarGift is not a direct transfer of the consumer's purchase price to a Creator. Any amount a Creator may later become entitled to receive is calculated separately under MedaStaré's Creator Program Terms and Creator Payout Schedule.

A StarGift is a digital virtual item made available by MedaStaré for purchase and use inside the MedaStaré ecosystem. It allows a user to express appreciation, recognition or support for an eligible Creator through a digital interaction within the Service.
